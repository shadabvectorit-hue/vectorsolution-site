About — the studio

A small studio with a precise way of working

VectorIT exists because too much custom software ships late, over budget, and owned by the wrong people. We run projects the way engineers wish they were run.

The name is the method. A vector has two properties: direction and magnitude. Software projects fail when they're missing one — plenty of effort pointed nowhere, or a clear goal with no momentum behind it. Everything about how we work is designed to keep both locked in: fixed scope so the direction can't drift, weekly shipped increments so the magnitude never drops to zero.

We're based in Austin, Texas, and we deliberately stay small. No account managers between you and the people writing your code. The person who scoped your project is the person building it — and the person answering the phone when something needs attention at 9pm before your launch.

Our roots are in operational software — logistics, dispatch, back-office systems where downtime costs real money and "it mostly works" isn't acceptable. That background shapes how we build everything: boring launches, documented handovers, and systems your team can actually run.
