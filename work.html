Flagship — Dual-entity ERP — Karachi · Dubai

A complete ERP for a two-country oil & gas business — built from scratch

GenCom Compression Services runs gas-compression packages for OGDC, PPL, Engro, Fauji Fertilizer and Mari Energies, operating as an ISO 9001:2015 company in Karachi and as an FZCO in Dubai. Both entities ran on Excel workbooks.

We built the GenCom Work Portal: an ERP covering the entire business in six workspaces — Sales & Distribution, Field Services, Finance & Accounts, HR & Payroll, an AI Workbench, and Settings — with strict separation between the Pakistani and Dubai entities, each on its own chart of accounts, tax regime and fiscal calendar.

It is not a set of forms over a database. The full export document chain runs inside it (Quotation → Proforma → Commercial Invoice → Packing List → Delivery Note), driven by six Incoterm-aware workflows of 14–21 stages each; a real double-entry general ledger sits underneath with eleven voucher types, trial balance, P&L and balance sheet; and multi-authority tax handling covers SRB, PRA, KPRA, federal GST and zero-rated foreign remittance — printing the formal nine-column tax invoice Pakistani law requires so buyers can claim input tax.

Inside the build — what it actually took

The parts that make it a real system, not a demo

Accounting software is easy to fake and hard to finish. These are the pieces that decide whether a business can actually close its books on it:

  • Two fiscal calendars — Pakistan's July–June year and the Dubai entity's calendar year, with document numbering that rolls on the tax year automatically
  • Money in local terms — amounts written in lakh and crore for PKR documents, international notation for USD
  • Field Cashier mobile app — outdoor staff photograph a receipt on site; it is read automatically, then an accountant approves it and the expense posts to the ledger against that person's cash float
  • Long-term contract billing — signed rate annexures stored per contract, so a release order is invoiced by picking agreed rates instead of retyping prices
  • Shipment tracking — one click to the live tracking page across eighteen airlines, couriers and shipping lines
  • Auditor-ready printing — every statement prints to A4 with the correct letterhead for whichever entity issued it

A full year of historical accounting was migrated out of spreadsheets and reconciled until the trial balance agreed to the rupee.

Inside the portal — shown with sample data

What the system actually looks like

Screens from the GenCom Work Portal, populated with sample figures. Real client data is never shown.

Sales & Distribution — Project Tracker

Every quotation and customer PO tracked from enquiry to delivery. The stage rail adapts to the Incoterm: a CPT export runs a different sequence to a domestic GST order, and each project shows exactly where it stands.

Finance & Accounts — Trial Balance

A real double-entry ledger: accounts grouped by class, running debit and credit totals, and a balance check that has to agree before the books close. Every statement exports to Excel or prints to A4.

Sales tax invoice — SRB format

The nine-column layout Pakistani law requires: value excluding tax, rate, tax amount and value including tax shown separately so the buyer can claim input tax. Amounts print in lakh and crore.

Manufacturing — Accounts + FBR — Karachi

Twenty years of books moved off one old PC — and straight onto FBR's digital invoicing

M. Ejaz Industries (Pvt) Ltd, a Karachi chemical manufacturer, ran its entire accounts — GST, excise, ledgers, invoicing — on a Visual Basic 6 program with an Access database that worked on a single ageing computer. We reverse-engineered it, migrated two financial years of data, and rebuilt it as a web application the team opens from any browser.

The new system keeps the menus the staff already knew, so training took a morning. Underneath: double-entry books on the July–June year, per-line GST and excise, gap-free sales tax invoice numbering, searchable accounts and customers, a sales commission report, Urdu notes on customer ledgers — and every invoice is reported to FBR Digital Invoicing under SRO 69(I)/2025 the moment it is saved, QR code included.

Paper trading — Stock + Accounts — Karachi

From reel to roll: a paper trader's stock and books in one system

Alliance Traders buys paper by the reel and sells it by the corrugated roll. Their old web portal held a trial balance and little else; stock lived in registers. We built an ERP that follows the paper: reels in by weight, rolls produced from them, sales priced by rate per inch and size, cartage on the same bill.

The owner's one condition was that the new statements must tick against the old portal line by line. So the trial balance, profit & loss and balance sheet print in the old portal's own layout — all 257 accounts agree to the rupee — while the new screens add what the portal never had: a live reel-stock panel grouped by quality and size, roll production, and customer and supplier ledgers.
