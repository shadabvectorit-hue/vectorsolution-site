Flagship — Dual-entity ERP — Karachi · Dubai

A complete ERP for a two-country oil & gas business — built from scratch

GenCom Compression Services runs gas-compression packages for OGDC, PPL, Engro, Fauji Fertilizer and Mari Energies, operating as an ISO 9001:2015 company in Karachi and as an FZCO in Dubai. Both entities ran on Excel workbooks.

We built the GenCom Work Portal: an ERP covering the entire business in six workspaces — Sales & Distribution, Field Services, Finance & Accounts, HR & Payroll, an AI Workbench, and Settings — with strict separation between the Pakistani and Dubai entities, each on its own chart of accounts, tax regime and fiscal calendar.

It is not a set of forms over a database. The full export document chain runs inside it (Quotation → Proforma → Commercial Invoice → Packing List → Delivery Note), driven by six Incoterm-aware workflows of 14–21 stages each; a real double-entry general ledger sits underneath with eleven voucher types, trial balance, P&L and balance sheet; and multi-authority tax handling covers SRB, PRA, KPRA, federal GST and zero-rated foreign remittance — printing the formal nine-column tax invoice Pakistani law requires so buyers can claim input tax.

Inside the build — what it actually took

The parts that make it a real system, not a demo

Accounting software is easy to fake and hard to finish. These are the pieces that decide whether a business can actually close its books on it:

  • Two fiscal calendars — Pakistan's July–June year and the Dubai entity's calendar year, with document numbering that rolls on the tax year automatically
  • Money in local terms — amounts written in lakh and crore for PKR documents, international notation for USD
  • Field Cashier mobile app — outdoor staff photograph a receipt on site; it is read automatically, then an accountant approves it and the expense posts to the ledger against that person's cash float
  • Long-term contract billing — signed rate annexures stored per contract, so a release order is invoiced by picking agreed rates instead of retyping prices
  • Shipment tracking — one click to the live tracking page across eighteen airlines, couriers and shipping lines
  • Auditor-ready printing — every statement prints to A4 with the correct letterhead for whichever entity issued it

A full year of historical accounting was migrated out of spreadsheets and reconciled until the trial balance agreed to the rupee.

Manufacturing — Accounting System — client name withheld

20 years of VB6 + Access accounting, rebuilt for the web — data intact

A Pakistani manufacturing SME ran its entire accounts — GST, excise, ledgers, invoicing — on a Visual Basic 6 desktop program with an Access database that only worked on one aging PC. We reverse-engineered the legacy system, migrated two decades of data, and rebuilt it as a modern web application their team uses from any browser.

What the new system does: full double-entry books (trial balance, P&L, balance sheet on Pakistan's July–June financial year), per-line GST and excise duty calculation, gap-free sales tax invoice numbering, four staff roles enforced on the server — and A4 print-perfect statements their accountant signs off on.
