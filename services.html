ERP Implementation — VectorERP, Odoo & ERPNext

Our flagship: getting your business off Excel and onto a real system. We have built a complete dual-entity ERP from scratch — general ledger, multi-jurisdiction tax, payroll and export documentation for a company operating in both Pakistan and Dubai. We implement our own VectorERP, and we implement and customize Odoo and ERPNext when they're the better fit — honest advice on which, before you spend.

Every implementation includes data migration, team training (Urdu or English), and go-live support. A shop is live in days; a multi-warehouse company in weeks.

  • Migration from Excel, registers & legacy software
  • FBR digital invoicing integration →
  • Training, documentation & ongoing support retainers
